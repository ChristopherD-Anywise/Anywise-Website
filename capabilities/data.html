Capability 04

Data.

Fragmented records, unproven outcomes, undetected risk.

Programme data usually arrives inconsistent, incomplete and too late to steer anything. We build the collection standards, detection engines and reporting pipelines that turn it into evidence you can put in front of a minister, an auditor or an executive board.

The problem

Reporting that satisfies the process but proves nothing.

Multi-site and multi-provider programmes generate enormous amounts of data and remarkably little evidence. Each site records what it can, in the format it chose, on the cycle that suits it. What arrives centrally is a compliance artefact rather than a management instrument.

The same gap hides risk. Where records are inconsistent, the patterns that indicate fraud or error are indistinguishable from ordinary variation, and manual review only ever reaches a fraction of the caseload.

  • 01

    Inconsistent collection

    Every site records differently, so nothing aggregates cleanly and comparison is guesswork.

  • 02

    Reporting lag

    Cycles measured in weeks mean the picture describes a programme that has already moved on.

  • 03

    Manual review ceilings

    Human screening can only cover so many cases, so the ones that never get looked at are invisible risk.

  • 04

    No evidence of impact

    Activity is easy to count. Whether it changed anything is the question that usually goes unanswered.

What we do

Standardise the input, automate the evidence.

We start at collection, because nothing downstream can be better than what comes in. Then we build the detection and reporting layers that make the resulting data continuously useful rather than periodically assembled.

01

Fix collection first

Standardised definitions and instruments across sites and providers, so what arrives can actually be compared and aggregated.

02

Build to your pipeline

Detection engines tailored to your data rather than a generic rules package, integrated with the systems already in place.

03

Rank the risk

Hierarchical classification puts the highest-risk cases at the top of the queue, so limited review capacity goes where it counts.

04

Automate the reporting

Dashboards that update continuously, cutting reporting cycles from weeks to days and turning compliance effort into insight.
