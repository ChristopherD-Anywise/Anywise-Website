About Anywise

Purpose-driven people, solving what matters.

Anywise is a wholly Australian-owned, veteran-owned, female-led B Corp. We mobilise high-performing teams to solve complex capability, technology and strategic challenges for defence and government, where the stakes are highest.

Our Purpose

Empowering people and organisations to create a positive impact in the world by enabling better decisions.

Our Values

Act Ethically & with Integrity We hold ourselves to the highest standards of honesty, transparency and accountability in everything we do.
Be Curious & Innovative We embrace continuous learning and creative problem-solving to deliver forward-thinking solutions.
Champion Empowerment & Collaboration We lift each other up, share knowledge freely and work together to achieve extraordinary outcomes.

Our People

any.one is welcome

We are a diverse, multidisciplinary team of engineers, analysts, veterans and specialists who care about the work as much as the craft. We bring lived operational experience together with deep technical expertise, and we build teams around the problem in front of us.

As a certified B Corporation, we hold ourselves to a higher standard of social and environmental performance, transparency and accountability, not just to our clients, but to our people and our community.
